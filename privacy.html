Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 August 2026

This policy explains what personal data Tradespeople collects, why we collect it, and what rights you have. We are the data controller for the information described here. We are established in Estonia and our service is aimed at people in the United Kingdom, so we handle personal data in line with both the EU GDPR and the UK GDPR (together with the UK Data Protection Act 2018).

Who we are

Tradespeople is operated by Unit 37 OÜ, a private limited company registered in Estonia (registry code 16961687), registered office Tornimäe tn 5, Kesklinna linnaosa, Tallinn, Harju maakond, 10145, Estonia. You can reach us at nathan.cavaglione@gmail.com.

Who this applies to

We deal with two groups of people:

  • Pros — the tradespeople who sign up and use the service.
  • Customers — the people who message a Pro about work. We process their data on the Pro’s behalf in order to run the Pro’s back office.

What we collect

  • Account details — your name, mobile number, trade, service area and working hours. We verify your number with a one-time code by SMS.
  • WhatsApp messages — if you connect your WhatsApp, the content of business conversations between you and your customers, including photos, voice notes, documents and phone numbers.
  • Calendar data — if you connect Google Calendar or iCal, your appointments, including titles, times and locations.
  • Accounting data — if you connect Xero or QuickBooks, invoices, price lists and related customer records.
  • Job and customer records — addresses, job notes, photos, prices and history we build up as work goes through the system.
  • Usage data — basic logs of how the app is used, for security and to improve the service.

How we use it

  • To reply to customer enquiries, schedule jobs and prepare quotes and invoices on your behalf.
  • To keep a record of your customers and past work.
  • To measure whether the service is actually saving you time and winning you work.
  • To keep the service secure, and to fix problems when they arise.

Our legal basis

  • Contract — most processing is needed to provide the service you have signed up for.
  • Legitimate interests — securing the service, preventing misuse, and improving how it works. We have considered the impact on you and keep this to what is reasonably necessary.
  • Consent — for connecting optional third-party accounts, and for any marketing messages. You can withdraw consent at any time.
  • Legal obligation — where we must keep records, for example for tax purposes.

Automated processing and AI

We use AI models to read incoming messages, summarise them and draft replies. During the pilot, drafts are reviewed by a person before anything is sent on your behalf, and jobs are only booked once you confirm. Message content may be sent to our AI provider for processing; providers used for this purpose do not train their models on your data. We do not make automated decisions that have a legal or similarly significant effect on anyone.

Who we share it with

We do not sell personal data. We share it only with service providers who help us run Tradespeople, under contracts that limit what they may do with it:

  • Cloud hosting and database providers.
  • AI model providers, for drafting and analysing messages.
  • Messaging providers, including WhatsApp/Meta and our SMS provider.
  • Calendar and accounting providers you choose to connect.
  • Professional advisers, or public authorities where the law requires it.

International transfers

Some of these providers are based outside the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom. Where data is transferred abroad we rely on an adequacy decision, or on Standard Contractual Clauses together with the UK International Data Transfer Addendum, so that it stays protected to an EU and UK standard.

How long we keep it

We keep your account and job records for as long as you use the service, and for up to 12 months after you close your account, unless we need to keep something longer for legal or accounting reasons (typically six years for financial records). You can ask us to delete your data sooner.

Security

Access to the system is limited to staff who need it, connections are encrypted in transit, and third-party account credentials are stored securely. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps and will notify you and the relevant supervisory authority about a breach where the law requires it.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask us to:

  • give you a copy of the personal data we hold about you;
  • correct data that is wrong or incomplete;
  • delete your data, or restrict how we use it;
  • send your data to another provider (portability);
  • stop processing based on legitimate interests, or withdraw consent.

Email nathan.cavaglione@gmail.com and we will respond within one month. If you are a customer of a Pro rather than a Pro yourself, contact the Pro in the first instance, or us and we will pass it on.

Complaints

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your data, you can complain to a supervisory authority. In the United Kingdom that is the Information Commissioner’s Office, ico.org.uk or 0303 123 1113. Our lead authority in the EU is the Estonian Data Protection Inspectorate (Andmekaitse Inspektsioon), aki.ee. You may also complain to the authority where you live or work. We would appreciate the chance to put it right first.

Cookies

This website sets no tracking or advertising cookies. The Tradespeople app uses a small number of strictly necessary cookies and similar storage to keep you signed in.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top, and tell you directly if the change is significant.
