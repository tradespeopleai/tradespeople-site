Tradespeople

The office manager you can’t afford.

Tradespeople is an AI back office for UK sole traders. It works inside the WhatsApp you already use — answering enquiries, booking jobs into your calendar and getting your invoices out, so you can get on with the job.

Join the pilot Currently running a limited beta with UK electricians, plumbers and handymen.

The problem

Most solo trades run their business on WhatsApp, a paper diary and a pile of disconnected apps. Leads fall through the cracks and admin bleeds into nights and weekends. Being the technician and running the business are two different jobs — and you’re doing both at once.

What it does

  • Answers your enquiries

    Connects to your WhatsApp and replies to new customers in minutes, not the next evening. It asks the right questions to size up the job before you’ve even seen the message.

  • Books the work in

    Reads your Google Calendar or iCal so it knows when you’re free, proposes realistic slots, and writes confirmed jobs straight back to your diary. Nothing gets booked without your say-so.

  • Handles quotes and invoices

    Syncs with Xero or QuickBooks. Raise the invoice from the job, send it on WhatsApp, and let it chase the ones that go quiet.

  • Remembers every customer

    Past jobs, addresses, photos and chat history in one place — so repeat customers get treated like repeat customers.

  • Takes voice notes

    “Invoice the Camden job, and tell my 2pm I’m running late.” Send it a voice note between jobs and it gets done.

Who it’s for

UK sole traders and small teams with plenty of call-outs — electricians, plumbers, gas engineers, appliance repair techs, handymen, boiler and heating engineers, and the trades either side of them.
